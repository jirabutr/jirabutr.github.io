The P-DTR® Framework

Most therapies focus entirely on the muscles and joints (the hardware). P-DTR focuses on the central nervous system (the software).

1. The Hardware

Bones, muscles, ligaments, and tendons. Traditional therapies massage or stretch these structures, but if the brain's signals are wrong, pain returns.

2. The Software

The proprioceptive system. Millions of receptors register touch, pressure, and stretch, sending data to the brain. P-DTR targets this stream.

3. The Reset

When trauma occurs, receptors become hypersensitive, firing false alarms. We safely locate these pairs and clear the glitch using swift, painless manual reflex stimulus.
